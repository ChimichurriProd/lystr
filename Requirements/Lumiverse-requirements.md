## 1. Background

Lumiverse is a new premium content studio and (eventually) streaming platform, formed as a subsidiary of In Your Head together with Joakim Lundell. The thesis: control the full value chain — develop IP, produce it, distribute it on owned channels (YouTube premium, paywalled plays, eventually lumiverse.se), and monetise through a mix of advertising, product placement, subscription, and sponsor partnerships. The reach lever is Jocke & Jonna's ~1.3M followers; the distribution lever is Lumiverse's own platform layer.

The immediate commercial need is **sponsor pitches**. Lumiverse is going into conversations with Google, Samsung, SJ, Strawberry and others to co-finance specific formats (starting with a Running Man–inspired format called *På rymmen / Flykten*). These conversations need a consistent, credible visual identity and a pitch artifact that works both on screen and on paper.

Lumiverse does **not** yet need a public-facing brand (no website, no external launch). What it needs is internal: a brand system and a working tool that makes every pitch look like it comes from the same serious company.

## 2. Brand direction

The customer has an existing visual seed: an AI-generated "tree-brain" image and a wordmark with a swoosh-style graphic element around the text. They like the typography of the current Lumiverse lockup (blockigt, sanseriff, lite "future") and want to keep building on it. They do **not** want gradients, particles, or heavy galaxy imagery as a default — those are too restrictive when the template has to work across 50+ slide layouts and printed output.

**Brand promise (working definition, to be refined):** Lumiverse is a distribution and production company that owns the full chain — creator, producer, distributor, audience. It is premium, modern, and operates across TV, streaming, YouTube, and social in a post-channel world.

**Value words:** not yet defined. See open questions.

## 3. Scope of this engagement

Three deliverables, tightly coupled. The PowerPoint template is the main working artifact — Alex (the person running Lumiverse operationally) uses slides as a daily working tool, not only as a pitch format. Everything flows from this constraint.

### 3.1 Brand identity / graphic profile ("lathund")

Starting from the existing tree-brain symbol and wordmark, develop a documented brand system Lumiverse can use consistently across pitches, internal docs, and future external material.

**Deliverables:**
- Logo refinement (symbol + wordmark lockup, based on existing direction)
- Color palette — primary + secondary, with explicit dark-mode and light-mode pairs
- Typography system (display, body, functional)
- Graphic language — rules for how the swoosh element is used, when imagery/galaxy/planet motifs are appropriate, and when they are not
- Brand guidelines document (PDF, ~10–15 pages)
- Digital asset kit (SVGs, PNGs, fonts, color tokens)

### 3.2 PowerPoint master template — light *and* dark variants

This is the primary deliverable. It is not a pitch deck; it is a **tool** Alex uses daily. Requirements driven directly by the meeting:

- Both **light** and **dark** variants, fully brand-consistent. Alex prints decks on paper and copies text in and out between apps — a dark-only template breaks his workflow.
- ~15–20 master slide layouts covering: cover, section divider, title + body, two-column, large image, image grid, quote, data/numbers, comparison, cast/talent intro, episode/format breakdown, sponsor integration, timeline, contact/sign-off.
- Reusable element library (icons, chart styles, callouts, dividers) embedded in the template so Alex can drop them in without leaving PowerPoint.
- Must work natively in PowerPoint (not exported-from-Figma images) so Alex can edit text, swap images, and duplicate slides without touching design software.

### 3.3 Mockup sponsor pitch deck — worked example

A populated example deck using the *På rymmen / Flykten* concept that the customer is actively pitching. This serves two purposes: (a) shows how the template works when filled out, (b) becomes the first real pitch Lumiverse uses (to Google Pixel and others).

**Deliverables:**
- ~20–25 slides, using the brand template
- Content adapted from the existing rough deck Alex walked through in the meeting (format overview, reach numbers, "telefon som format-centrum" Google-specific mechanics, hotel safe-house mechanic for Strawberry, train mechanic for SJ, résuméer from Jocke's prior productions, audience/reach data)
- Image sourcing — stock or AI-generated imagery, replaceable by Lumiverse later
- Notes on which slides are "swap-per-sponsor" vs. "fixed structure"

## 4. Deferred / conditional work

These are explicitly post-hook — the customer said "de lägger vi inte tid och pengar på" until a sponsor has committed in principle.

- **3D renders / visualisations** of product placement scenarios (e.g., a contestant using a Google Pixel AI camera function to swap backgrounds, a Samsung phone centre-frame, a Billys pizza integration)
- **Bespoke per-sponsor pitch variants** (full redesign of the mockup deck for each sponsor after a first positive signal)
- **Motion / animated logo reveal** for use in produced content
- **Teaser / sizzle reel** — short video montage for sponsor meetings
- **External-facing brand extension** — website, social, public launch assets

We recommend reviewing these once the first sponsor signal comes in, and re-scoping as a second engagement.

## 5. Out of scope (this engagement)

- No public website (lumiverse.se is explicitly "på sikt", not now)
- No social media presence setup
- No video/trailer production
- No Jocke & Jonna personal brand work (that lives on their existing YouTube channels)
- No In Your Head corporate brand update (Lumiverse is the focus; In Your Head is the parent)

## 7. Open questions — need to be answered before sign-off

### 7.1 Value words / brand attitude (Section 2)
The customer ("Alex") said he had not yet landed the value words. Before visual direction can be finalised, we need a short alignment exercise:
- Who is Lumiverse *not*? (Easier to define than "who is Lumiverse".)
- What are 3–5 existing brands Lumiverse wants to feel adjacent to? (A24 and Miramax were referenced as structural analogues — owning a recognisable creative voice. This is a starting point, not a conclusion.)
- What is the one thing a sponsor should feel after closing the deck?

**Proposal:** one 60-minute working session with Alex to land value words before brand concepting begins.

### 7.2 Accounts & tooling ownership (applies across deliverables)
Similar to the Lyster engagement, we assume Sriracha sets things up using Lumiverse's own accounts. This needs confirmation:
- Does Lumiverse have its own Microsoft 365 / PowerPoint licensing, Dropbox, and any font/stock accounts, or will these be set up as part of this project?
- Who holds credentials during and after the project?
- Post-project: does Alex / In Your Head own the master template file outright, and can it be freely shared internally across In Your Head, Lumiverse, and Sriracha-adjacent collaborators?

**Our recommendation:** Lumiverse owns all accounts and the master template file. Sriracha retains editable source files (Figma, AI projects) and delivers PowerPoint natively editable. No subscriptions held by Sriracha on Lumiverse's behalf.

### 7.3 Working environment
- The team uses Dropbox primarily (confirmed in meeting). Confirm Dropbox is where deliverables land.
- PowerPoint is the required output format for the template (not Keynote, not Google Slides). Confirmed in meeting — noted for the record.

### 7.4 Content rights for the mockup deck
The mockup deck will reference real potential sponsors (Google, Samsung, SJ, Strawberry). These are *pitch artifacts* and will be shown to those sponsors themselves — so logo use and product imagery are contextually appropriate. However:
- Is Lumiverse comfortable with placeholder use of sponsor logos on the mockup slides, understanding it is pitch-only and never public?
- For the *Flykten* format creative itself (concept, format mechanics) — who owns the IP? We assume Lumiverse / In Your Head; please confirm.

### 7.5 Jocke & Jonna imagery
The mockup deck uses Jocke & Jonna as on-screen talent. We assume Sriracha can use existing publicity photos of them under Lumiverse's rights as co-owners. Please confirm before we source imagery.

### 7.6 Timeline (Section 6)
- What is the actual deadline?
- Is there a specific sponsor meeting we are targeting as the "ready by" moment?
- Phased delivery acceptable (brand first, template second, mockup third) or all together?

### 7.7 Rights & usage
Same as the Lyster engagement:
- **Buyout:** Lumiverse owns final assets outright. Recommended.
- **License:** Sriracha retains ownership; Lumiverse licenses for defined uses.

**Our recommendation:** full buyout, Sriracha retains portfolio and case-study rights (with the understanding that any reference to Jocke & Jonna in Sriracha's portfolio needs separate approval — celebrity likeness).

Additional:
- Font licensing — who holds the license (Lumiverse)?
- Stock imagery used in the mockup deck — who pays and who holds the license?
- Editable source files (Figma, AI) — delivered to Lumiverse or retained by Sriracha?

- Two rounds of revisions per deliverable. Third round or more billed hourly at 1 000 SEK/h.
- Alex is available for weekly 30–45 min check-ins and turns around feedback within 3 business days.
- The customer does not need external-facing brand extension (web, social, public launch) as part of this engagement.

- Fonts are licensed for PowerPoint/document use. Any web/video font licensing is out of scope here.
