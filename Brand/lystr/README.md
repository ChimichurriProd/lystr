## Quick summary of what we found

**Logo:** single wordmark "Lystr®" in a custom-feeling grotesque. Two live variants (white-on-black favicon, white-on-transparent for dark backgrounds). One deprecated gradient variant (pink→purple→blue) that Alex has explicitly rejected for the new brand — *"Det är en signal om att vi leker. Vi leker inte. Vi ska vara solida, robusta, trovärdiga."*

**Colors:** an 11-stop grayscale ramp plus two accents — red `#E6414F` and soft green `#CCE4BF`. No primary brand hue beyond the red. Feels unfinished rather than minimal — no defined system.

**Typography:** Schibsted Grotesk (primary, weights 400–900 + italics) and Fragment Mono (monospace). Both free/open-source.

**Imagery:** a mix of product installation shots (houses with solar panels) and stock lifestyle imagery leaning heavily on golden-hour Nordic nature. May not align with the target audience's taste — flag for discovery.

**Copy:** functional, technical, number-heavy. Strong on mechanics of the offer; weak on emotional/narrative hook. Two visible typos on the live site ("Avlsuta", "Clensun") worth flagging in kickoff.

## Key observations for the new engagement

1. **This is a prototype, not a system.** Nothing here is load-bearing for the new brand — we have freedom to define from scratch.
2. **The gradient is out.** Explicit guidance from Alex.
3. **Copy inconsistency between site and sales pitch** — 5–8 år / 65–80% (site) vs 8 år / ~65% (meeting). Align early.
4. **Brand hierarchy is unresolved.** Lystr + Cleansun relationship is stated inconsistently ("En del av Clean Sun" in title, "En del av Clensun" in footer, full Cleansun AB registration visible). Decide in brand discovery.
5. **Photography style may not fit the real audience.** Too stock-y for the Grimslöv-family positioning described in the meeting. Flag.
6. **No brand guidelines exist.** What's live is whatever got applied to Framer. Starting fresh is lower-risk than trying to extend the existing system.
